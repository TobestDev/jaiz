Jaiz Bank Plc Public Offer — Terms & Conditions

1. Data Usage Consent

By proceeding, you consent to the collection, processing and use of your personal information for the Jaiz Bank Plc Public Offer share subscription, in accordance with the Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR) and applicable law.

2. Information Collected

  • Full name, date of birth, gender
  • Bank Verification Number (BVN)
  • Contact details (phone, email, address)
  • Bank account details (for dividend payments)
  • CSCS / CHN details (if applicable)

3. Purpose of Data Processing

  • Identity verification through BVN or account validation
  • Processing your share application
  • Crediting shares to your CSCS account
  • Processing dividend payments
  • Regulatory compliance and reporting

4. Offer Terms

  • You are 18 years or older (or applying through a parent/guardian for a minor)
  • Minimum subscription is 100 units, in multiples of 100, at ₦1,000.00 per unit
  • Allotment is at the discretion of Jaiz Bank Plc and the issuing houses
  • Shares are credited electronically to your CSCS account
  • Refunds (if any) are paid to your designated account
